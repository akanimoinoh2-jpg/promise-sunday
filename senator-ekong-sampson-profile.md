# Senator Ekong Sampson, Ph.D — A Concise Profile

## Biography

Senator Ekong Sampson, Ph.D, is a journalist, author, poet, publisher, legal practitioner and public servant whose career reflects intellectual depth, impactful leadership and an enduring commitment to public service and human capital development.

Born on September 21, 1966, he hails from Mkpat Enin Local Government Area of Akwa Ibom State. His academic journey began with a Distinction-grade Ordinary National Diploma (OND) in Mass Communication from The Polytechnic, Calabar (1986). He proceeded to law, earning an LL.B from the University of Cross River State (1990), the Barrister-at-Law (BL) qualification from the Nigerian Law School, Lagos (1991), an LL.M from the University of Lagos (1999), and crowning these with a Ph.D in Law from the University of Calabar (2020).

His professional life began in legal practice — with Pat Okupa & Co., Benin City (1992–1996), and as Managing Partner of Abalu, Bobga, Sampson & Co., Ikeja, Lagos (1998–2000). His legislative acumen then earned him appointment as Chief Legislative/Special Assistant to Senator Udoma Udo Udoma, then Chairman of the Senate Committee on Appropriations (2000–2003).

Senator Sampson's elective career took off in 2003 with his election as Councilor and Chairman of Mkpat Enin Local Government Area, serving two terms until 2007. He represented Mkpat Enin State Constituency in the Akwa Ibom State House of Assembly from 2007 to 2015, emerging as one of its most visible and respected lawmakers. He subsequently served in the Akwa Ibom State Executive Council (2015–2020) across several strategic portfolios: Commissioner for Rural Development; Economic Development, Labour and Manpower Planning; Environment and Petroleum Resources; and Special Adviser in the Bureau of Political and Legislative Affairs and Water Resources — distinguishing himself throughout with administrative competence and a people-centred approach to governance.

In February 2023, he was elected to represent Akwa Ibom South Senatorial District in the 10th Senate of the Federal Republic of Nigeria. He is known for his simplicity, humility, accessibility and vast knowledge base. He is happily married to Mrs. Matina Sampson, a senior staff member of the National Assembly, and their union is blessed with children.

## Current Roles

- **Senator**, Akwa Ibom South Senatorial District, 10th National Assembly (since June 2023)
- **Chairman**, Senate Committee on Solid Minerals Development
- **Vice Chairman**, Senate Committee on Basic and Secondary Education
- **Member**, several other strategic Senate committees
- **Chairman**, Akwa Ibom National Assembly Caucus

## Legislative Record

Sponsored significant bills and motions, including:

- Establishment of the **Federal University of Technology, Ikot Abasi**
- **Federal Medical Centre Act (Amendment)**
- Establishment of the **Federal University of Medical Sciences, Eket**
- **Solid Minerals Development Corporation**
- **South South Development Commission**

Widely respected in the Senate for legislative depth, incisive contributions and forward-looking perspectives.

## Empowerment & Human Capital Development

- Facilitated employment for **over 200 Akwa Ibom youths**; federal appointments for **more than 50 constituents** since 2023
- Supported individuals and communities with vehicles, scholarships, grants, land, medical assistance and welfare packages, especially for the less privileged
- Initiated and championed the construction of the **PDP Secretariat, Mkpat Enin** — regarded as one of the finest local government party secretariats in Nigeria

## Education & Intellectual Advocacy

- Sponsors the **monthly meeting of the Uyo Book Club**, a vibrant intellectual community promoting reading and scholarly engagement
- Established **Excellence College, Mkpat Enin (2014)**, which provided tuition-free education to many students for several years before its takeover by the Akwa Ibom State Government
