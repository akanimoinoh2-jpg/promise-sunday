# Senator Ekong Sampson, Ph.D — Fact Sheet: Senate Accomplishments & Interventions

## Appointments

- Chairman, Senate Committee on Solid Minerals
- Vice Chairman, Senate Committee on Education (Basic & Secondary)
- Formerly, Vice Chairman, Senate Committee on FCT
- Chairman, Akwa Ibom National Assembly Caucus
- Member of a number of other Senate Committees
- Member, Senate Ad Hoc Committee to Investigate Abuse of Firearms by Officers of Nigeria Customs
- Member, Senate Ad Hoc Committee to Investigate Alleged Illegal Annexure of Nigerian Mangrove Communities by the Republic of Cameroun
- Member, Senate Ad Hoc Committee to Investigate Illegal Oil Theft in Nigeria

## Bills

- **Federal Medical Centre, Akwa Ibom South** — Bill as part of the FMC Act Amendment, set for implementation
- **Federal University of Medical Sciences, Eket** — set for Second Reading
- **Federal University of Technology, Ikot Abasi** — set for Third Reading
- **Nigerian Solid Minerals Development Corporation** — First Reading
- **Child Rights Act Amendment Bill** — First Reading
- **South South Development Commission** (co-sponsor) — Passed and Assented
- **Mining Sites Remediation and Reclamation Bill** — First Reading, among others

## Motions (Sponsored & Co-sponsored)

- Urgent need for the reconstruction of Calabar–Itu Highway
- Need for increased security surveillance along Akwa Ibom Coastal Highway
- Urgent need to investigate alleged annexation of Nigerian mangrove communities by the Republic of Cameroun
- Notification on the transition of Senator Joseph Wayas, former President of the Senate
- Call for urgent rehabilitation of Federal Government roads across the country
- Matter of Urgent National Importance: the hijack of a boat and abduction of passengers, including prospective JAMB candidates, by sea pirates along the Calabar–Oron waterways
- Many additional bills and motions filed and awaiting consideration

## Petitions

- Presented **over 9 petitions** on behalf of constituents; two led to reinstatement into Federal Government employment
- Mediated alternative settlements for many constituents with their employers

## Facilitation of Federal Government Employment & Training

- Facilitated employment and reinstatement for **over 60 indigenes** of Akwa Ibom South and Akwa Ibom State into top agencies, parastatals and uniformed organizations
- Facilitated human capacity development training for **over 1,000 indigenes** in mining, media, ICT and other fields

## Empowerment

- **Mega empowerment programme** within his first six months in office — over **1,000 beneficiaries**, mostly youth and women, with education and business support grants and car gifts
- Second tranche of the empowerment billed for later this year, with improved packages and increased beneficiaries
- **₦100 million grant** to students in Akwa Ibom South
- Additional **₦10 million** for Law Students from Akwa Ibom South in Nigerian Law Schools
- Free medical outreach underway
- Periodic donations of cars, tricycles and motorcycles to supporters and constituents
- **Over 50 physical projects** facilitated to Akwa Ibom South

## Other Advocacies

Key advocate for the:

- Establishment of the South South Development Commission
- Revitalization of ALSCON
- Construction of roads in Akwa Ibom South
- Establishment of the Federal University of Agriculture, Abak, Akwa Ibom State
- Ibom Deep Seaport
- Completion of Nkari Dam

## Working Collaborations

- Active collaboration with the Senate President, Senator Godswill Akpabio; Governor Umo Eno; fellow National Assembly members; Ministers; relevant MDAs; and stakeholders across Akwa Ibom South and beyond
- Widely respected in the National Assembly as a bridge builder of deep intellect, range and maturity
