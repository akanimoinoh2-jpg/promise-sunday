Akwa Ibom South Senatorial District

Senator Ekong Sampson

A concise fact sheet of accomplishments and interventions in the Senate of the Federal Republic of Nigeria.

Fact sheet·Profile

01 Appointments & Roles

  • Chairman, Senate Committee on Solid Minerals
  • Vice Chairman, Senate Committee on Education (Basic & Secondary)
  • Formerly, Vice Chairman, Senate Committee on FCT
  • Chairman, Akwa Ibom National Assembly Caucus
  • Member of a number of other Senate Committees
  • Member, Senate Ad Hoc Committee to Investigate Abuse of Firearms by Officers of the Nigeria Customs
  • Member, Senate Ad Hoc Committee to Investigate Alleged Illegal Annexure of Nigerian Mangrove Communities by the Republic of Cameroun
  • Member, Senate Ad Hoc Committee to Investigate Illegal Oil Theft in Nigeria

02 Bills

  • Bill for the establishment of a Federal Medical Centre in Akwa Ibom South — passed as part of the FMC Act Amendment; set for implementation
  • Bill for the establishment of a Federal University of Medical Sciences, Eket — set for Second Reading
  • Bill for the establishment of a Federal University of Technology, Ikot Abasi — set for Third Reading
  • Nigerian Solid Minerals Development Corporation Bill — First Reading
  • Child Rights Act Amendment Bill — First Reading
  • South South Development Commission Bill (co-sponsor) — passed and assented
  • Mining Sites Remediation and Reclamation Bill — First Reading, among others

03 Motions (sponsored & co-sponsored)

  • Urgent need for the reconstruction of the Calabar–Itu Highway
  • Need for increased security surveillance along the Akwa Ibom Coastal Highway
  • Urgent need to investigate the alleged annexation of Nigerian mangrove communities by the Republic of Cameroun
  • Notification on the transition of Senator Joseph Wayas, former President of the Senate
  • Call for the urgent rehabilitation of Federal Government roads across the country, among many others
  • Matter of Urgent National Importance: the heart-rending hijack of a boat and abduction of passengers — including prospective JAMB candidates — by sea pirates along the Calabar–Oron waterways
  • Many bills and motions are also filed and awaiting to be taken

04 Petitions

  • Over 9 petitions presented for constituents
  • Two petitions led to reinstatement into Federal Government employment for the concerned constituents
  • Used his good office to mediate alternative settlements for many constituents with their employers

05 Facilitation of Federal Government Employment

True to his well-known love for sustainable human capital development, Senator Ekong Sampson has facilitated employment and reinstatement for over 60 indigenes of Akwa Ibom South and Akwa Ibom State into top agencies, parastatals and uniformed organisations.

He has also facilitated human capacity development training for over 1,000 indigenes of Akwa Ibom South — in areas including mining, media, ICT, among others.

06 Empowerment

  • A mega empowerment programme held within his first six months in office had over 1,000 indigenes of Akwa Ibom South as beneficiaries — mostly youths and women. Many received education and business support grants, while some constituents received cars
  • A second tranche of the empowerment is billed to hold later this year, with improved packages and increased beneficiaries
  • Provision of a ₦100 million grant to students in Akwa Ibom South
  • Announcement of an additional ₦10 million for Law students from Akwa Ibom South in the Nigeria Law Schools
  • Senator Sampson's massive free medical outreach is equally underway this year
  • Periodic donation of quality car gifts, tricycles and motorcycles to supporters and constituents
  • Over 50 physical projects facilitated to Akwa Ibom South

07 Other Advocacies

Senator Ekong Sampson has been a key advocate for the:

  • Establishment of the South South Development Commission
  • Revitalisation of ALSCON
  • Construction of roads in Akwa Ibom South
  • Establishment of the Federal University of Agriculture in Abak, Akwa Ibom State
  • Ibom Deep Seaport
  • Completion of the Nkari Dam

08 Working Collaborations

  • The Onomkpoinam Mkpat Enin enjoys active collaboration with the Senate President, Senator Godswill Akpabio; Governor Umo Eno; fellow National Assembly members; Ministers; relevant MDAs; and a wide strata of stakeholders in Akwa Ibom South and beyond
  • Widely respected in the National Assembly as a bridge builder of deep intellect, range and maturity
