01 Overview

Senator Ekong Sampson, Ph.D, is an accomplished journalist, author, poet, publisher, legal practitioner and public servant whose career has been defined by intellectual depth, impactful leadership and an enduring commitment to public service and human capital development.

Born on September 21, 1966, Senator Sampson hails from Mkpat Enin Local Government Area of Akwa Ibom State. His academic journey reflects a relentless pursuit of excellence.

02 Education

  • OND in Mass Communication with Distinction — The Polytechnic, Calabar (1986)
  • LL.B — University of Cross River State (1990)
  • Barrister-at-Law (BL) — Nigerian Law School, Lagos (1991)
  • Master of Laws (LL.M) — University of Lagos (1999)
  • Doctor of Philosophy (Ph.D) in Law — University of Calabar (2020)

03 Legal & Legislative Career

  • Pat Okupa & Co., Benin City — legal practice (1992–1996)
  • Managing Partner, Abalu, Bobga, Sampson & Co., Legal Practitioners, Ikeja, Lagos (1998–2000)
  • Chief Legislative/Special Assistant to Senator Udoma Udo Udoma, then Chairman of the Senate Committee on Appropriations (2000–2003)

04 Elective Politics & the Executive Council

  • Councilor and Chairman, Mkpat Enin Local Government Area — elected 2003; served two successful terms until 2007
  • Member, Akwa Ibom State House of Assembly — represented Mkpat Enin State Constituency (2007–2015); distinguished himself as one of the Assembly's most visible, active and respected lawmakers
  • Akwa Ibom State Executive Council (2015–2020), holding several strategic positions:
    • Commissioner for Rural Development
    • Commissioner for Economic Development, Labour and Manpower Planning
    • Commissioner for Environment and Petroleum Resources
    • Special Adviser, Bureau of Political and Legislative Affairs and Water Resources
  • Across these portfolios, he demonstrated exceptional administrative competence and a people-centred approach to governance

05 Education & Human Capital Development

  • Sponsor of the monthly meeting of the Uyo Book Club — a vibrant intellectual community that promotes reading and scholarly engagement
  • Founder, Excellence College, Mkpat Enin (2014) — a notable educational institution that provided tuition-free education to many students for several years before its eventual takeover by the Akwa Ibom State Government
  • Throughout his public life, he has earned a reputation as a quiet champion of empowerment and human capital development — facilitating employment opportunities for over 200 Akwa Ibom youths
  • Since assuming office as Senator in June 2023, has facilitated Federal Government employment for more than 50 constituents
  • Supported numerous individuals and communities through the donation of vehicles, scholarships, grants, parcels of land, medical assistance and welfare packages — particularly to party faithfuls and the less privileged
  • Initiated and championed the construction of the People's Democratic Party Secretariat in Mkpat Enin LGA — regarded as one of the finest local government party secretariats in Nigeria

06 In the Senate

In February 2023, Senator Ekong Sampson was elected to represent Akwa Ibom South Senatorial District in the 10th Senate of the Federal Republic of Nigeria.

He currently serves as Chairman of the Senate Committee on Solid Minerals Development, Vice Chairman of the Senate Committee on Basic and Secondary Education, and is a member of several other strategic committees. He is also the Chairman of the Akwa Ibom National Assembly Caucus.

As a legislator, he has distinguished himself as a widely respected voice in the Senate, noted for his legislative depth, incisive contributions and forward-looking perspectives. He has sponsored several significant bills and motions, including:

  • Establishment of the Federal University of Technology, Ikot Abasi
  • Federal Medical Centre Act (Amendment)
  • Establishment of the Federal University of Medical Sciences, Eket
  • Solid Minerals Development Corporation
  • South South Development Commission, among others

Known for his simplicity, humility, accessibility and vast knowledge base, Senator Ekong Sampson remains a respected statesman and an influential figure in Nigeria's legislative and political landscape.
